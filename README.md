# Large-Foundation-Models-for-Question-Answering-in-Autonomous-Driving
# Survey and Review Papers

## Overview

This section collects representative survey and review papers related to large foundation models and their applications in autonomous driving and intelligent transportation systems.

The collected surveys cover a broad range of topics, including large language models (LLMs), vision-language models (VLMs), multimodal large language models (MLLMs), foundation models for autonomous driving, world models, vision-language-action (VLA) models, and multi-agent systems.

To facilitate navigation, the papers are organized into two categories:

- **Autonomous Driving and Intelligent Transportation:** Surveys focusing on foundation models, large language models, multimodal understanding, and their applications in autonomous driving and intelligent transportation systems.
- **General Foundation Models and Supporting Surveys:** Surveys on general-purpose LLMs, multimodal models, reasoning, hallucination, and multi-agent systems that provide useful background for autonomous driving QA research.

## Notes

### Relevance

- **Core:** Directly addresses foundation models, LLMs, VLMs, or MLLMs in autonomous driving and intelligent transportation.
- **Related:** Covers closely related technologies, such as world models, VLA models, occupancy perception, and generative driving systems.
- **Supporting:** Provides general background on LLMs, multimodal reasoning, agents, evaluation, safety, and other enabling technologies.

### Publication Information

- The year and venue refer to the publication information or the first public preprint version, as indicated in the table.
- For papers with both preprint and peer-reviewed versions, the final publication information should be checked and updated.
- **The paper titles currently link to Google Scholar search results.** These links provide a way to locate the corresponding publications; they are not direct publisher or arXiv links.
- Official publisher, DOI, arXiv, or project links can replace the search links once the corresponding records have been verified.
- Papers unrelated to foundation models, autonomous driving, intelligent transportation, or supporting QA technologies are not included in this curated list.
